The Problem We're Solving

Students struggle to find compatible study partners and coordinate effective group sessions, leading to missed learning opportunities and academic isolation. Study Sesh bridges this gap.

Why Study Sesh?

🤝 Enhanced Learning

Unlock powerful peer-to-peer learning opportunities that enhance understanding and retention.

⚡ Streamlined Coordination

Effortlessly schedule sessions, book rooms, and coordinate with your study group—all in one place.
